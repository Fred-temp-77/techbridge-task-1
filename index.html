Bridging Learning to Real-World Experience

What are we about?

TechBridge by Baselink Services Limited is a company that offers remote internships designed to help beginners transition from simply having the knowledge to having real, practical experience. They offer an opportunity to work on real-world style projects, improve skills and create experiences, as well as offer a structured and supportive WhatsApp community of fellow interns.

Our Programs

Web Development

Ready to turn your foundational knowledge into functional projects? Through this internship, one can move past tutorials to build responsive web applications through structured individual and team assignments. This fast-paced sprint is designed to polish one's coding skills, teach one real-world troubleshooting, and help them create an impressive, job-ready portfolio piece that proves one can build for the real web.

Data Analysis

Put your basic data skills to work on practical, real-world problems. With this program, one can tackle hands-on data tasks—either independently or with peers—to clean, analyze, and visualize datasets. They will sharpen their analytical thinking, learn how to extract meaningful insights, and walk away with a compelling portfolio project that shows employers they can translate raw data into business value.
